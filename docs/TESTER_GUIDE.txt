TAILORTOWN - PLAYTEST GUIDE
===========================

Thanks for testing. This takes about an hour, and the game keeps notes for you.


1. PLAY
-------
- Start a New Game and play at least three in-game days. More is welcome.
- Play the way you normally would. Skipping the tutorial, going broke or losing
  customers are all useful to us. There is no wrong way to play.
- Save and quit from the pause menu (Esc) when you stop.

While you play, the game quietly records how each day went: customers, orders, cloth
you bought, money, and how often customers said no at the mirror. It doesn't record
anything about you or your computer beyond the graphics card and window size.


2. SEND THE REPORT
------------------
When you're done:

  Esc  ->  Playtest Report  ->  Copy to Clipboard

Then paste it into your message to us. Or press "Open Folder" and attach the
report_....txt file instead.

The file is also here, if the game won't open:
  %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\TailorTown\playtest\
(Paste that into the Windows Explorer address bar.)

The report saves itself every minute, so after a crash just send whatever is in
that folder.


3. TELL US IN A FEW LINES
-------------------------
Short answers are fine. We read every one.

- When were you bored, and when were you rushed?
- Did a customer ever turn down a suit in a way that felt unfair?
- Was there anything you couldn't work out how to do?
- Any bugs: what you were doing, and what happened. A screenshot helps.

And the one thing you'd change first.
