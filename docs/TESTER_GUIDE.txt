TAILORTOWN - PLAYTEST GUIDE
===========================

Thanks for testing. It takes about an hour, and the game keeps notes as you play.


1. PLAY
-------
- Start a New Game and play through the tutorial with Mr. Hemming.
- Then run your own shop for at least three in-game days. More is welcome.
- Play the way you normally would. Going broke or losing customers is useful to
  see too. There is no wrong way to play.
- Save and quit from the pause menu (Esc) when you stop.

While you play, the game records how each day went: customers, orders, cloth
bought, money, and how often customers said no at the mirror. Apart from that it
only notes your graphics card and window size.


2. SEND THE REPORT
------------------
When you're done:

  Esc  ->  Playtest Report  ->  Copy to Clipboard

Paste it into your message. Or press "Open Folder" and attach the report_....txt
file instead.

The file is also here, if the game won't open:
  %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\TailorTown\playtest\
(Paste that into the Windows Explorer address bar.)

The report saves itself every minute, so after a crash just send whatever is in
that folder.


3. ANSWER WHAT YOU CAN
----------------------
Short answers are fine. Skip any question you have no opinion on.

The tutorial
- Did Mr. Hemming explain things in the right order?
- Was there a step where you didn't know what to do next?
- Too long, too short, or about right?

The minigames (cutting, sewing, ironing, coffee)
- Which one did you enjoy most, and which least?
- Were any too hard, too easy, or unclear about what counted as good work?
- Did any start to feel repetitive by day three?

Customers
- Did a customer ever turn down a suit in a way that felt unfair?
- Did the dress codes (what suits a wedding, a funeral, and so on) make sense?

Money and cloth
- Did you ever run short of money? When?
- Did cloth prices and suit prices feel fair?
- Did you end up buying cloth you never used?

Pacing
- When were you bored, and when were you rushed?

Menus and controls
- Was any menu hard to read or find your way around?
- Did any control feel awkward? Keyboard or gamepad?
- Was anything on screen too small, too crowded, or easy to miss?

The look
- What did you think of the art style overall?
- How did the finished suits look on the customers?
- Could you tell the fabrics and patterns apart?
- Anything that looked broken, flickered, or seemed out of place?

Bugs
- What you were doing, and what happened. A screenshot helps.

Last one: what's the first thing you'd change?
