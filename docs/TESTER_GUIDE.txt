TAILORTOWN - PLAYTEST GUIDE
===========================

Thanks for testing. The game keeps notes as you play, so all you need to do is play
and answer a few questions at the end.


1. PLAY
-------
- Start a New Game and play through the tutorial with Mr. Hemming.
- Then run your own shop for as long as you like.
- Play the way you normally would. Going broke or losing customers is useful to
  see too. There is no wrong way to play.
- Save and quit from the pause menu (Esc) when you stop.

While you play, the game records how each day went: customers, orders, cloth
bought, money, and how often customers said no at the mirror. Apart from that it
only notes your graphics card and window size.


2. SEND THE REPORT
------------------
When you're done:

  Esc  ->  Playtest Report  ->  Copy to Clipboard

Paste it into your message. Or press "Open Folder" and attach the report_....txt
file instead.

The file is also here, if the game won't open:
  %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\TailorTown\playtest\
(Paste that into the Windows Explorer address bar.)

The report saves itself every minute, so after a crash just send whatever is in
that folder.


3. ANSWER WHAT YOU CAN
----------------------
Short answers are fine. Skip anything you have no opinion on.

1. The tutorial: was there a step where you didn't know what to do, or a part
   that went on too long?

2. The minigames (cutting, sewing, ironing, coffee): which did you like most and
   least, and were any too hard, too easy, unclear or repetitive?

3. Customers and dress codes: did the briefs make sense, and did a customer ever
   turn down a suit in a way that felt unfair?

4. Money and pacing: did prices feel fair, did you run short of money, and when
   were you bored or rushed?

5. Menus and controls: was anything hard to read, hard to find, too small or
   awkward to use (keyboard or gamepad)?

6. The look: what did you think of the art style, and of the suits on the
   customers? Could you tell the fabrics and patterns apart?

7. Bugs: what were you doing, and what happened? A screenshot helps.


4. YOUR OWN TAKE
----------------

8. Which part did you enjoy more: fixing up and renovating the shop, or designing
   and making suits? Would you play the game for one without the other?

9. What would you most like to see added, and what's the first thing you'd
   change?

10. What games does this remind you of, and what kind of game would you want it
    to become?
